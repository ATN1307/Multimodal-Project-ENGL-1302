Travel Agencies/Bloggers' Point of View:

The travel bloggers/agencies believe without a dout that travel positively affects a person's communication. Throughout the webpages, I went over the points of loneliness, isolation, and anxiety, and talked about how the travel bloggers believe that travel betters communication by reducing those aforementioned feelings. A big reason as to why the travel bloggers think this way is because of their of definition of empathy. Travel bloggers define empathy as connecting with and understanding their new environment and its people. This definition of empathy promotes the act of socializing as it tells a person to focus on connecting with the environment. By focusing on trying to care and connect with the people in their new enviroment, it provides a person with plenty of real-life scenarios where they can practice their communication skills. This practice allows a person to not only hone their physical skills in communication, but also their mental skills. Encounteting all these new people, cultures, and customs, without any preparation is gauranteed to make a person anxious. What if I risk offending someone? or I don't know what kind of person they are the usual thoughts that might run through a person's head when trying to encounter all these new things. Trying to connect with the locals can help ease a person's anxiety as it allows a person to create a feeling of familiarity with locals. This can help a person avoid feeling out of place and anxious as familiarity establishes trusts which allows a person to find like minded individuals they can relate too. This belief that a person's communication can be bettered by travel, is due to the travel bloggers defining empathy as focusing on trying to connect with and understand the enviroment they are in.

Pyschologists' Point of View:

On the hand of the spectrum, we have the psychologists. Travel forces people to encounter new customs, enviroment, languages, and people without any preparation. This lack of preparation causes people to feel anxious and alone. During these times of anxiousness and loneliness, a person usually has a support systems of friends and family they can rely on. Without this support system, they don't have anyone to rely on which can cause them to fall into a loop of loneliness and anxiety which can eventually lead a person to give up on communicating as a whole. Because of the aforemetioned reasons, the psychologists believe that travel negatively affects a person's communication. The reasons of anxiety and loneliness listed by the psychologists can be explained by their definition of empathy. Psyschologists define empathy as caring for and nurturing existing connections, not replacing them with new ones. By stripping a person away from their support system, they are left to navigate this new place by themsleves without having anybody to trust. Without their trust system, a person can start to feel out place because they have no one to confide in. This lack of trust can also make it difficult for a person to connect with the locals because there is always that thought of "Can I really trust this person ?", this thought process can lead to a dry and unintenionally offensive person. However, if said person were to have a friend with them, it would make interacting with the locals of this new place a less anxious task because they have someone to fall back on if things do go wrong. In addition to this, having a friend around while you're trying to understand this new place makes a person feel less lonely because their also going through the same thing.


Review of the Psychologists Points:

  • Loneliness and Isolation #1 : Travel can hinder communication by putting people in unfamiliar environments, which can lead to feelings of isolation and loneliness, because they start to feel disconnected from their usual support systems.
  • Anxiety #2 :By forcing a person out of their circle of trust, travel can induce communication-related anxiety as they become more distrustful of others.
  • Definition of Empathy #3: Psychologists define empathy as caring for and nurturing existing connections, not replacing them with new ones every single time you travel.

Review of the Travel Bloggers/Agencies Points:

  • Loneliness and Isolation #1 : Travel can reduce feelings of loneliness by pushing a person into random social interactions, which develop communication by giving you chances to try out different communication skills.
  • Anxiety #2 :Travel can reduce communication-related anxiety by constantly forcing them into social interactions, this repetitin acts as practice and preparation which helps a person treat communication as a mundane routine not a dreadful task.
  • Definition of Empathy #3: Travel bloggers define empathy as connecting with and understanding their new environment and its people.
