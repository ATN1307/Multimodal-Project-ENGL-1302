Loneliness and Isolation:

Travel Agencies/Bloggers' Point of View:

The travel agencies/bloggers believe that travel can develop a person's communication by not allowing them to isolate themselves or feel lonely. Travel allows people to experience new cultures and explore the landscapes of their new environments. Part of this exploration involves communicating with the locals and isolating yourself from them. If a person wants to get something or get something done, they're going to have communicate that to the locals. Travel agencies/bloggers believe that travel can develop a person's communication skills by forcing them out of their comfort zone. Staying in your comfort zone can lead to feelings of loneliness and isolation because you are not engaging with the world around you. Travel forces people to engage with the world around them, which can help them develop their communication skills by giving them more practice communicating with others. This constant practice can help breakdown the language barriers and shift the dynamics from 2 strangers trying to communicate to 2 people trying to connect, being able to connect with someone eliminates the feelings of loneliness and isolation because you have someone to talk to and share your experiences with.

“As we explore busy streets, peaceful landscapes, and lively markets, we open up to new connections and experiences. Travel lets a person meet people from different cultures and backgrounds, which helps break down barriers between people. These interactions teach people how to communicate effectively. Whether learning a new language, sharing cultural exchanges, or swapping stories with other travelers, travel sharpens our communication skills.” -Dhow Khasab Tours, 2024

Psychologists' Point of View:

The first point made by the psychologists is that travel hinders communications by causing loneliness and isolation. By taking a person and into a country thounsands of miles away, they are separated from their support systems, friends, family members, etc. and must navigate new social environments. Not having anybody to talk to in this new environment can lead to feelings of loneliness because the person will feel like their is no one around for them to talk. This feeling of loneliness can also lead to a feeling of disconnection or isolation, which makes communicating with the locals unpleaseant as a person might act more dry and unengaing which can lead to an improper conversation where they might end up unintentionally offending the locals. The fear of offending someone is also a concern that leads people to avoid conversations with others in a new environment. Travel forces people to encounter new cultures, languages, and customs, but it doesn't give you a proper way to prepare for these interactions. This lack of preparation can exacerbate a person's feeling of loneliness because they are unable to overcome all these barriers. This can further develop the feeling of loneliness which causes a person to show "lower levels of functioning" in other words, they become less engaging, less social, and depressed, which can cause people to give up on communicating as a whole by hiding themselves from the public. And just as a butcher constantly sharpens his knife to get a clean cut, communication skills work the same way. Loneliness leads to isolation, which can cause people to miss out on the constant practice that helps sharpen their communication skills.

“Another common experience among students who study abroad is loneliness. Since students are typically removed from their usual social support systems when they study abroad, they may feel they have few people on whom to rely…Students experiencing more psychological distress and more loneliness demonstrated lower levels of functioning while studying abroad.” -Science Direct, 2021

Conclusion:

Even though travel hinders communication by causing loneliness and isolation, it more so helps develop a person's communication skills by forcing them to engage with the world around them. To say that travel hinders communication by breeding feelings of loneliness and isolation isn't wrong, but it is a very narrow perspective. Travel can cause feelings of loneliness and isolation, but it more often than not helps people overcome those feelings by giving them the opportunity to get out of their comfort zone and engage with the world around them. Regardless of if the person is scared of offending someone or not, they will have to communicate with someone to get something done, and this constant practice develops a person's communication skills by teaching them how to engage in a conversation effectively with people in different social situations. In addition, it also helps a person create similar support system as this constant practice helps a person overcome the language barriers and connect with the locals, which eliminates the feelings of loneliness and isolation because they will have found someone to trust and talk to in this new environment.
