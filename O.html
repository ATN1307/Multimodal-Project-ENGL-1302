Multimodal Project Overview




Research Topic:

My research for this semester, is centered around does travel have a positive or negative effect on communication? When I ask this question, I don't just want a “Yeah, it negatively affects communication, mate”, I want to know what effects it has on a person. Does it make a person awkward and narrow-minded like me, or does it make a person more confident and open-minded because the only way to feel like an outsider is to try to communicate with locals.

Stakeholders:

This class, a Stakeholders can be a person, group, or entity and is defined as people who are invested in or are effected by the issue at hand. For this research project, I chose to go over both its negatives and positives. Because of this, I chose to have a stakeholder to represent the positve effects of travel on communication and the negative effects of travel on communications. On the positive side, my stakeholders are: Travel agencies/bloggers and people in the education field. On the negative side my stakeholders psychologists A reason I chose them as my stakeholders is because not only have they seen a person's communication get effected by travel, but they also provided the perfect balances between mental and physical developments. By that I mean both stakeholders talk about a person's physical behavior and their mental behavior.

Stakeholder Overview:

The stakeholders in this research project represent different perspectives on the effects of travel on communication. The positive stakeholders include travel agencies, bloggers, and individuals in the education field who emphasize the positive affects travel of travel on a person's communication skills. The negative stakeholders are psychologists who focus on the drawbacks of travel has on communication, such as increased anxiety and loneliness, which can hinder effective communication. To breifly summarize both perspectives, the positive stakeholders argue that travel enhances communication by pushing a person out of their commfort zone and making them making them practice making new connections, while the psychologists contend that travel hinders communication by doing gthe exact opposite, instead of fostering new connections, it pushes a person away from their existing support systems which leads to feelings of isolation and anxiety, which may impede communication.

Project Goal:

My goal for this project is to persuade my primary audience the psychologists that travel doesn't hinder communication, rather it develops it by offering more chances to practice commnication and building up a person's self-esteem. Through the research I also want to see if I able to convince myself that travel is something that develops a person's communication not hinder it.
